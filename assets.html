Why this is a short list and not a rebuild

Findings 1, 2 and 4 are configuration. They are an afternoon, and they are the reason the content you have already paid for is invisible. Findings 3 and 5 are writing. Nothing on this list requires the site to be rebuilt, and we would not propose rebuilding it.

The order matters more than the list. There is no sense putting money into traffic while thirteen pages are telling Google to index something else.

You paid for fourteen pages. One of them is allowed to rank.

That is a configuration fault, not a content problem, and it is the cheapest thing on this list to fix.
